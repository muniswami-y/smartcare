# CareSmart Nurse Quick Reference Guide

This guide covers triage vitals recording and inpatient Medication Administration (MAR) workflows.

---

## 1. Recording OPD Triage & Inpatient Vitals
1. Log in and navigate to **Nurse Vitals**.
2. Select the patient from the list on the left.
3. Input physiological parameters:
   - Blood Pressure (Systolic / Diastolic in mmHg)
   - Pulse Rate (bpm)
   - Temperature (°F)
   - SpO2 Oxygen Saturation (%)
   - Respiratory Rate (/min)
   - Weight (kg) and Height (cm) (BMI calculates automatically)
4. **Abnormal Alerts:**
   - The system automatically triggers red banners for Hypertension, Hypoxia, Pyrexia, or Tachycardia.
5. Click **Save Vitals to Electronic Chart**.

---

## 2. Inpatient Medication Administration (MAR)
1. Switch to the **Medication Chart (IPD)** tab.
2. The list displays all scheduled medications ordered by treating physicians.
3. To record administration:
   - Click **✓ Administer** when the dose is given.
   - The system records the exact administration timestamp and your nursing ID.
4. If a dose is missed or refused:
   - Click **Missed Dose**.
   - Select or enter the clinical reason (e.g., *Patient vomited / Patient in Radiology for CT Scan*).
   - Click **Confirm Missed Dose**. The attending doctor is notified automatically.
