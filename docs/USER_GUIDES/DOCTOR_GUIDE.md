# CareSmart Doctor Quick Reference Guide

Welcome to CareSmart, Doctor! This guide explains how to manage your daily OPD queue, write e-prescriptions, and order investigations.

---

## 1. Accessing Your OPD Queue
1. Log in at `https://hospital.caresmart.in/login` with your hospital credentials.
2. Click **Doctor Consult** in the top navigation bar.
3. Your queue displays on the left, sorted by Token Number (#01, #02, etc.).
4. Click on a patient to open their clinical workspace.

---

## 2. Reviewing Vitals & Clinical History
- The top header shows demographics, age, gender, and **KNOWN ALLERGIES** highlighted in red.
- Review nursing triage vitals (BP, Pulse, Temperature, SpO2, and calculated BMI).
- Any abnormal readings (e.g. BP > 140/90, SpO2 < 95%) appear with emergency flags.

---

## 3. Clinical Notes & E-Prescribing
1. Enter **Chief Complaints**, **History of Present Illness**, and **Primary Diagnosis**.
2. To prescribe medications:
   - Type the medicine name (e.g., *Paracetamol 650mg* or *Azithromycin 500mg*).
   - Select dosage, frequency (e.g., *1-0-1 Twice daily*), duration in days, and special instructions.
   - Click **+ Add Rx**.
3. **Safety Warnings:**
   - If the patient has a documented allergy to the drug or class, a **CRITICAL ALLERGY ALERT** appears.
   - If a duplicate active prescription exists, a warning is raised.
   - If a warning appears, enter your clinical override rationale before finalizing.

---

## 4. Ordering Diagnostic Tests
- Select laboratory or imaging investigations from the catalog dropdown (e.g. *Complete Blood Count (CBC)*, *Chest X-Ray PA View*).
- Click **+ Add Order**. Orders are transmitted instantly to Laboratory and Radiology worklists.

---

## 5. Finalizing & Printing
- Click **Save Draft** at any time to autosave your notes.
- Click **Lock & Conclude Visit** to finalize the consultation. This locks the notes, transmits medication orders to the Pharmacy, and synchronizes charges with Billing.
- Click **Download / Print Rx** to generate a signed digital prescription PDF.
