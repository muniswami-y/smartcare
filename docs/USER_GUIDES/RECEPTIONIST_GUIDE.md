# CareSmart Receptionist & Cashier Quick Reference Guide

This guide covers patient registration, appointment booking, OPD check-in, and billing counter operations.

---

## 1. Patient Registration & Search
1. Open **Reception & OPD**.
2. Type patient name, mobile phone number, or Patient ID (`CS-XXXXXX`) in the search bar.
3. If the patient is not found, click **Register New Patient**:
   - Enter Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Mobile Number, and Residential Address.
   - For minors, select Guardian relationship.
   - Click **Save Patient Record**. The system assigns a unique permanent Patient Code.

---

## 2. OPD Appointment Booking & Walk-in Tokens
1. Select the treating Doctor and desired date.
2. Select an available time slot, or click **Walk-in Token** for same-day walk-in consultations.
3. The system generates a tamper-proof daily sequential token number (e.g. #01, #02).
4. When the patient arrives at the hospital, click **Check-in**. The patient's token is immediately visible on the doctor's consult screen and waiting lounge screens.

---

## 3. Cashier Desk & Payments
1. Open **Billing**.
2. Select the patient's active bill. All charges from OPD consultation, lab tests, and dispensed medications are consolidated automatically.
3. Apply any approved fee concessions (mandatory reason required).
4. Enter payment amount and choose payment mode:
   - **UPI:** Scan dynamic QR code (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm).
   - **Cash:** Collect physical cash at the till.
   - **Card:** Swipe/tap debit/credit card via POS terminal.
5. Click **Receive Payment & Generate Receipt**.
6. Print the official computerized money receipt PDF for the patient.
