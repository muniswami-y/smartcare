# CareSmart Radiologist Quick Reference Guide

This guide explains how to view diagnostic imaging studies, utilize medical image viewer tools, and author signed reports.

---

## 1. Diagnostic Imaging Worklist
1. Log in and open **Radiology**.
2. Select a study from the worklist (e.g. Chest X-Ray, Brain NCCT, Abdominal Ultrasound).

---

## 2. Using the Medical Image Viewer
- **Zoom & Pan:** Click Zoom In/Out or use mouse wheel to examine fine structures.
- **Rotation & Invert:** Rotate in 90° increments; toggle Invert to switch between bone and soft-tissue polarity.
- **Brightness & Contrast Sliders:** Adjust dynamic range dynamically.
- **Measurement Calipers:** Click the Caliper tool and click two points on the image to measure anatomical distance in millimeters.
- **Source Notice:**
  - If the study was captured via mobile camera or film scan, note the mandatory statutory banner: *"Notice: Photograph / scan of film is for quick reference only. The signed radiology report remains the primary diagnostic authority."*
- **DICOM Fallback:** For full 3D multi-planar reconstruction (MPR) or volumetric slice stacks, click **Download Raw DICOM (.dcm)** to open in local workstation OsiriX / Horos / Radiant viewers.

---

## 3. Reporting & Digital Sign-off
1. Review image findings in the viewer.
2. Click **Edit & Sign Report**.
3. Enter **Detailed Findings** and **Final Impression**.
4. Click **Apply Digital Signature & Lock**. The report is locked into the immutable record and synchronized to the patient's portal.
