# CareSmart Patient & Family Portal User Guide (English)

Welcome to the CareSmart Patient Portal! Access your family's verified medical records, live appointment tokens, and itemized bills directly from your mobile device.

---

## 1. How to Log In
1. Visit `https://hospital.caresmart.in/patient/login` or tap the CareSmart PWA app on your phone.
2. Enter your 10-digit registered mobile phone number.
3. Tap **Send 6-Digit Verification OTP**.
4. Check your SMS messages and type the 6-digit OTP code.
5. Tap **Verify & Enter Portal**.

---

## 2. Managing Your Family
- Use the dropdown in the top-right corner to toggle between family members (Self, Spouse, Children, Elderly Parents).
- You can view health records and prescriptions for all family members linked under your registered phone number.

---

## 3. Live Token Tracker
- Tap **Live Token & Wait Time**.
- View your live OPD token number (e.g., #08), the token currently in the doctor's room (e.g., #05), and estimated wait time in minutes.
- Head to the doctor's waiting lounge when your token is near.

---

## 4. Viewing Signed Health Records & Reports
- Tap **Health Records Timeline**.
- View completed doctor visit summaries, signed lab test reports, and imaging reports.
- Tap **Download PDF** to save official digital copies to your phone or share with your family doctor.

---

## 5. Transparent Bills & Online Payments
- Tap **Bills & Online Payments** to view an itemized breakdown of every test, consultation, and pharmacy item.
- Tap **Pay Online (UPI / Card)** to settle balances via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Credit/Debit card.

---

## 6. Your Privacy Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
- Tap **Privacy & DPDP Rights**.
- **Transparent Access Log:** Check "Who Viewed My Data" to see every hospital doctor or nurse who opened your record, with timestamp and purpose.
- **Data Portability:** Tap **Request Complete Health Data Archive** to download all medical records in an open digital format.
- **Lock Sensitive Records:** Toggle the lock on confidential health records.
